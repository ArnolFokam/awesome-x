# Opportunities Relevant for Africas

A curated list of online ML, AI, NLP, and data science communities with opportunities relevant for Africa.

## Communities

| Community | Location | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [AFIRM-Africa](https://groups.google.com/g/afirm-africa) | Africa | Google Group for African information retrieval researchers, referenced in connection with the ACM SIGIR Low Resource Environments track. |
| [AFRICAI](https://africai.org/) | Africa | Pan-African network and MICCAI special interest group advancing African-led medical imaging AI research, education, and collaboration. |
| [African Artificial Intelligence Institute](https://groups.google.com/g/african-artificial-intelligence-institute) | South Africa | Google Group referenced in a South African AI ethics landscape study mailing; no clearly matching public website could be verified. |
| [AfriClimate AI](https://africlimate.ai/) | Africa | Grassroots community using AI to address climate change challenges across Africa through research, workshops, and outreach. |
| [AI4D Africa](https://africa.ai4d.ai/) | Africa | IDRC- and Sida-supported program building responsible, inclusive AI research, policy, and talent across Africa. |
| [AIMS Chat Community](https://nexteinstein.org/) | Africa | AIMS-wide chat mailing list used to share digital transformation, data, and AI community announcements. |
| [BioNLP](https://groups.google.com/g/bionlp) | Global | Mailing list for the biomedical and clinical NLP research community, often used for shared tasks and workshop announcements. |
| [Black in AI](https://blackinai.org/) | Global | Nonprofit affinity community increasing Black representation in AI through workshops, mentorship, travel grants, and advocacy. |
| [Centring Data in African AI](https://centring-data-in-african-ai.github.io/workshop/) | Africa | Deep Learning Indaba workshop community focused on African data ownership, licensing, and ethical data stewardship in AI. |
| [Computational Sociology](https://groups.google.com/g/computational-sociology) | Global | Google Group for discussion of computational sociology and institutionalizing computational social science. |
| [Data Science Africa](https://www.datascienceafrica.org/) | Africa | Pan-African nonprofit running summer schools and workshops to build data science, ML, and AI capacity for African challenges. |
| [Deep Learning Indaba](https://deeplearningindaba.com/) | Africa | Grassroots pan-African organisation and annual conference strengthening machine learning and AI across the continent. |
| [Deep Learning IndabaX Botswana](https://deeplearningindaba.com/2024/indabax/botswana/) | Botswana | Locally organised Deep Learning IndabaX chapter building ML and AI capacity in Botswana. |
| [Deep Learning IndabaX South Africa](https://indabax.co.za/) | South Africa | South African IndabaX conference and community spreading machine learning knowledge through tutorials, posters, and hackathons. |
| [{Dis}Ability in AI](https://elesa.github.io/ability_in_AI/) | Global | NeurIPS-affiliated affinity group supporting and advocating for disabled people in AI and ML conferences. |
| [DSA Uganda Chapter](https://www.datascienceafrica.org/uganda/dsa-uganda-chapter) | Uganda | Data Science Africa local chapter fostering peer support and knowledge exchange among Ugandan data scientists. |
| [Data Science for Social Impact (DSFSI)](https://www.dsfsi.co.za/) | South Africa | University of Pretoria research group advancing African language NLP and data science for societal impact. |
| [Forum for Information Retrieval Evaluation (FIRE)](https://fire.irsi.org.in/) | India | Annual evaluation forum and conference for information retrieval and NLP research, with shared tasks and peer-reviewed tracks. |
| [Gambia Data Science & Machine Learning](https://groups.google.com/g/gambia-data-science--machine-learning) | The Gambia | Google Group for Gambian data science and machine learning community announcements; public web presence could not be independently verified. |
| [Hundzula NLP <> Linguistics Retreat](https://sites.google.com/view/hundzula-retreat) | Africa | Interdisciplinary retreat connecting linguists and NLP researchers to advance African language technologies. |
| [Indigenous in AI](https://indigenousinai.org/) | Global | NeurIPS affinity community connecting Indigenous AI and ML researchers and elevating Indigenous voices in technology. |
| [ISICNI Alumni](https://groups.google.com/g/isicni-alumni) | Global | Alumni Google Group referenced in a cross-posted opportunity mail; no public website or description could be verified. |
| [International Society for Scientometrics and Informetrics (ISSI)](https://www.issi-society.org/) | Global | International scholarly society for scientometrics and informetrics, with biennial conferences and an official mailing list. |
| [LatinX in AI (LXAI)](https://www.latinxinai.org/) | Global | Global volunteer community creating opportunities for LatinX researchers through workshops, mentorship, and GPU access programs. |
| [LoResMT](https://www.loresmt.org/home) | Global | Recurring ACL-affiliated workshop on machine translation for low-resource and under-represented languages. |
| [Machine Learning News](https://groups.google.com/g/ml-news) | Global | Moderated Google Group for ML community announcements including events, calls for papers, and jobs, maintained by IMLS. |
| [Masakhane African Languages Hub](https://www.linkedin.com/company/masakhane-african-languages-hub/) | Africa | Masakhane initiative building open, community-driven datasets and language AI projects for African languages. |
| [Masakhane Leadership](https://www.masakhane.io/home) | Africa | Internal Masakhane leadership Google Group coordinating governance and community decisions for the Masakhane NLP movement. |
| [Masakhane](https://www.masakhane.io/home) | Africa | Grassroots pan-African NLP community strengthening research in African languages through open, participatory projects. |
| [ML Collective Africa](https://mlcollective.org/wiki/mlc-africa/) | Africa | Pan-African early-career ML and AI research community supporting mentorship, skill-building, publication, and conference participation. |
| [Machine Learning for Health (ML4H)](https://ml4h.cc/) | Global | Community around the annual ML4H symposium on machine learning research for health and biomedicine. |
| [Machine Learning and Data Science Africa Network (MLDS Africa)](https://groups.google.com/g/mlds-africa) | Africa | Google Group network connecting African ML and data science researchers, practitioners, and announcements. |
| [North Africans in NLP](https://sites.google.com/view/NorthAfricansInNLP) | Africa | Affinity group for North African NLP researchers organising socials and panels at major NLP conferences. |
| [Queer in AI](https://www.queerinai.com/) | Global | Nonprofit community making AI safe and inclusive for LGBTQIA+ researchers through workshops, Slack, and advocacy. |
| [R-Ladies Gaborone](https://www.meetup.com/rladies-gaborone/) | Botswana | Gaborone chapter of R-Ladies Global promoting gender diversity in the R community through meetups and workshops. |
| [SAICSIT](https://saicsit.org/) | South Africa | South African Institute for Computer Scientists and Information Technologists professional society and members mailing list. |
| [SIGANN](https://sigann.github.io/) | Global | ACL special interest group on linguistic annotation of language resources, sponsoring the annual Linguistic Annotation Workshop. |
| [SIGHUM](https://sighum.wordpress.com/) | Global | ACL special interest group on language technologies for the humanities, social sciences, and digital cultural heritage. |
| [SIGMORPHON](https://sigmorphon.github.io/) | Global | ACL special interest group on computational morphology and phonology, running annual workshops and shared tasks. |
| [SIGTYP](https://sigtyp.github.io/) | Global | ACL special interest group promoting linguistic typology and its integration with multilingual NLP. |
| [SisonkeBiotik](https://www.sisonkebiotik.africa/) | Africa | Open grassroots community building African capacity in machine learning and AI for health through participatory research. |
| [SisonkeBiotik Discord](https://discord.gg/YKDeK2QeUB) | Africa | Discord server for real-time discussion and collaboration within the SisonkeBiotik community. |
| [WiNLP (Widening NLP)](https://www.winlp.org/) | Global | ACL-affiliated workshop community promoting diversity and supporting underrepresented voices in natural language processing. |
| [Women in Machine Learning (WiML)](https://www.wiml.org/) | Global | Volunteer organisation supporting women in machine learning through mentorship, workshops, and a public directory. |
| [Zindi](https://zindi.africa/) | Africa | Africa's largest platform for data science competitions, learning, and hiring focused on real-world AI challenges. |
